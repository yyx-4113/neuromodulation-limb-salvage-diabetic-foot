## What changed in the numeric-claim audit

The five numeric claims (C1-C5) all cite [3] alone. [3]'s EPMC abstract was checked on 2026-11-18: it enumerates non-pharmacological DFU modalities but contains **no** SCS mention and **none** of 70% / 60-80% / 40-60% / 30-50%. Its full text is independently confirmed closed by Unpaywall (`is_oa=False`), OpenAlex (`oa_status=closed`) and OpenAIRE (no candidate repository URL).

Numerically close values **do** exist in open full texts, but in a different population: [42] 79% at 3 months (PDN RCT, PMID 33818600), [43] 59% at 6 months (PDPN RCT, PMID 25216508), [47] 90.1% at 24 months (PDN follow-up, PMID 37536514). These are logged as **candidate origins only**, not as verified sources, because the consensus claim concerns diabetic foot while all three trials enrolled painful diabetic neuropathy without ulcer/ischemia and reported pain—not amputation—endpoints.

The one open DF-specific controlled study, [4] (retrospective cohort, PMID 38334381, 102 vs 104), reports 12-month total amputation OR 0.17 (95% CI 0.08-0.37), but allocation was by patient willingness; it is cited in the foreword, not attached to C5.
